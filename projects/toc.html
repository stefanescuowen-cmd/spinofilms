Project

The Tale of Corruption

A sci-fi dystopian novel and planned first animated series for SpinoFilms, written and directed by Owen Stefanescu.

TOC Section

At the dawn of time, the Great Hands of Thimos created ten planets and formed Galeous. Peace ended when Sephon Morgenstern, under Lucifer Mercer, seized control. After Sephon was sealed away, a new threat caused chaos in Masamatsu. Seiji, a half-elf from Masamatsu, must prove himself worthy of the Kurai Shuen and decide whether he can overcome the corruption.
