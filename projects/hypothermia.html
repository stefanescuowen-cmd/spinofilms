Franchise

The Hypothermia Series

Hypothermia is a Star Wars-inspired series following James, Master Coni, and the conflict between the Eskimo Empire and Yeti Empire.

Franchise Notes

James trains to become an Eskimo Knight under Master Coni. Mr. Berg, the false chancellor of the Eskimo Empire, is revealed to be a Yeti Lord. After a mission ends with James injured, James becomes enraged at Coni and joins the Yeti Empire to destroy him.

  • Universe code: 4924
  • Known sequel: Hypothermia II, The Eskimos Strike Back
