Franchise

The Finch Series

Originally a parody of The Grinch, The Finch Series later grew into a lore-heavy Christmas franchise.

Franchise Notes

The story follows teenagers who do not like Christmas in a neighborhood where almost nobody celebrates it. Mr. Finch tries to make Christmas a holiday, fails, and eventually starts hating Christmas himself. Later, Professor Ingus Pingus's Tryposyrum makes Finch more powerful and more animalistic as he tries to get revenge on the teenagers.

  • Universe code: 1989
  • Status: guardian killed
  • Related entries: Mr. Finch, Professor Ingus Pingus, Tryposyrum
