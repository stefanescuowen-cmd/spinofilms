About SpinoFilms

SpinoFilms is an independent film production group founded by Ronan Luu in 2019.

What We Do

Born out of a passion for storytelling and creative exploration, SpinoFilms brings together filmmakers, writers, artists, and content creators dedicated to producing original films across different genres. From action-packed trilogies to comedies and specials, the group keeps pushing its independent filmmaking forward.

SpinoFilms focuses on the full production process, from concept development to filming and post-production. Projects include short films, web series, feature-length ideas, and story worlds with a strong emphasis on character development, visual ideas, creativity, and teamwork.

Ronan continues to lead the group as founder and director, guiding many of the movies while helping shape the overall direction of SpinoFilms.
