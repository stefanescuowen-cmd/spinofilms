Members

Meet the Team

These bios are based on the original SpinoFilms website. Photo placeholders are ready for approved casual member photos.

Ronan Photo

Ronan Luu

Founder / Director

Ronan, also known as Mr. President, is the founder of SpinoFilms and the company director for most movies. He also acts in several projects and often helps with photography and camera work.

Chi Photo

Chi Luu

Actor

Chi is Ronan's younger brother and often appears as comic relief, a child character, a monster, or even a villain in projects like The Hunt.

Logan Koch Photo

Logan Koch

Veteran Cast Member

Logan is a veteran member best known for playing Grandpa in skits and older movies. He still occasionally appears in anniversary specials.

Owen Photo

Owen Stefanescu

Secondary Director / Actor / Website Administrator

Owen acts as a secondary director and has connected projects such as The Tale of Corruption. He is known for slapstick humor, voices, expressive performances, and creating websites including this one.

Adriangely Photo

Adriangely Morales-Castillo

Illustrator

Adriangely is an artist whose role is important for The Tale of Corruption, especially as the comic book series develops.

Monarcane Photo

Logan "Monarcane" Klinedinst

Artist / Filmmaker

Monarcane is a secondary artist who loves filmmaking, works on analog horror, created concept art for The Tale of Corruption, and made the SpinoFilms logo.

Jake Photo

Jacob "Jake" Magrecki

Cameraman / Actor

Jake is often the cameraman and appears in Easter eggs. He is also known for funny acting, recognizable voices, and facial expressions.

Zoe Photo

Zoe Magrecki

Camera / Actor

Zoe, Jake's twin sister, often works camera and appears in several movies with sarcastic and witty humor.

Jared Photo

Jared Kong

Tech / Actor

Jared is SpinoFilms' tech guru, bringing drones, speakers, cameras, and gear to filming days. He also plays major roles including Professor Ingus Pingus.

Derek Photo

Derek Irwin

Editor / Actor

Derek is a train-loving member with editing skills and roles such as Benson in The Hunt and the Bounty Hunter in Hypothermia II.

Christian Photo

Christian

Retired Member

Christian supported the early days of SpinoFilms and acted in projects such as The Apocalypse.

Alaina Photo

Alaina

Retired Member

Alaina starred in various short films and Apocalypse movies, often clashing with Logan Koch in those stories.

Serenity Photo

Serenity Myers

Actor

Serenity appeared in The Last Stand as Josiah, the main character trying to save Hogking City from Cyan Daily.

Max Photo

Max Myers

Actor

Max, Serenity's younger brother and Chi's best friend, appeared as a member of Cyan's army in The Last Stand.
