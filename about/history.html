History

History of SpinoFilms

A timeline of the group's evolution from Indoraptor Films into SpinoFilms, plus the movie release history from the original site.

Evolution of SpinoFilms

March 2019 - June 2019

Originally known as Indoraptor Films, the group began after exploring iMovie and creating its first project, The Lost World, later retitled The Prehistoric Island.

June 2019 - August 2019

Production grew quickly with projects like The Indo Trilogy, The Porgs, the Godzilla Trilogy, and Nightmare Fox News. Logan and Owen joined during this era.

August 2019 - February 2020

The group collaborated with Extencia, attended monthly meetings, met members like Christian and Alaina, and leaned further into comedy films.

March 2020 - February 2021

The COVID-19 pandemic interrupted production, delaying or canceling projects and slowing progress.

March 2021 - September 2021

The group reorganized, adopted the SpinoFilms name, moved away from unoriginal ideas, and added Adriangely while Owen developed new concepts.

October 2021 - August 2022

The renaissance period introduced projects like Paruresis and Trypophobia, with Jake, Zoe, Jared, Serenity, and Max joining the team.

September 2022 - Present

SpinoFilms entered a phase of steadier releases, creative growth, and renewed production momentum.
